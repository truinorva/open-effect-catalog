## How to attribute

Keep `LICENSE` (or a link to it) with the data or with a product built on it,
as the MIT License requires. On an imprint, an "Open-source licences" page or
in an About box, one line is enough:

> Open Effect Catalog — © 2026 Truinorva GmbH, Robert Adunka (TRIZ Consulting
> Group) and contributors, MIT License.

German version:

> Open Effect Catalog unter MIT-Lizenz, Copyright 2026 Truinorva GmbH,
> Robert Adunka (TRIZ Consulting Group) und Mitwirkende.

The `authors` field stays in every entry as data; you need not display it.

## Where the first entries come from

The first 51 effects, 3 applications and the vocabulary (actions, objects,
operations, parameters) were written by Robert Adunka and his co-authors for
the **TRIZ Effects Database** of TRIZ Consulting Group,
https://www.triz-consulting.de/EffectsDatabase/ (data version 2026-11-11,
vocabulary version 2.1.1). There they are published under CC BY 4.0. Robert
Adunka, TRIZ Consulting Group, permitted their use in this repository under
the MIT License.

They were converted into one YAML file per entry and one vocabulary file;
wording and cell allocations were not changed at import. Entries taken over
this way carry `origin: triz-consulting`. All later changes are recorded in
the git history.
